# User Guide: Patient Health Portal Operations

## 1. Getting Started
The Patient Health Portal is a secure, progressive web application designed to give you complete visibility and control over your personal health records.

## 2. Registration and Authentication
1. **Registration**:
   - Provide your full legal name, date of birth, mobile number, and password.
   - Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number for identity verification (stored securely with masked display `XXXX-XXXX-1234`).
   - Verify ownership of your mobile number by entering the 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) dispatched to your phone.
2. **Mediclaim ID**:
   - Upon successful registration, you receive a unique, permanent 12-digit Mediclaim ID (e.g. `3048 4019 6339`).
   - Keep this ID handy; doctors and hospitals use it to request access to your records.

## 3. Managing Clinical Records

### 3.1 Viewing Your Health Timeline
- The **Timeline** provides a continuous, chronological stream of your medical history, including past consultations, immunizations, and diagnoses.
- Use category filters to quickly view specific records (e.g., Allergies, Medications, Reports).

### 3.2 Uploading Medical Documents
1. Click **Upload record** on your Dashboard.
2. Select an image (PNG, JPG) or PDF of your prescription, discharge summary, or lab report.
3. The automated extraction engine reads the document using AI OCR and presents the extracted clinical items for your review.
4. Verify or adjust the extracted dosages, dates, and instructions, then click **Confirm & Save**.

### 3.3 Exporting Your Record History
- To download your complete medical history, click **Download history** on the Dashboard or Records page.
- The portal generates an official, high-resolution **PDF document in a clean tabular format** detailing your demographics, emergency contacts, and complete medical history.

## 4. Controlling Doctor Access
1. When a clinician requests access, an alert appears under **Access Requests**.
2. Review the requesting doctor's name, medical specialty, requested record types, and access duration.
3. Click **Approve** to grant temporary access or **Decline** to deny the request.
4. You can view all currently active doctor sessions under **Active Grants** and click **Revoke** at any time to instantly revoke access.
